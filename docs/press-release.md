**Telarchy Launches Platform That Uses Prediction Markets to Align AI Agents With Your Goals**

*The startup introduces "capitalism for alignment," a new approach to keeping AI working in your interest*

**San Francisco, CA, March 16, 2026** - Telarchy, Inc. today announced the public launch of its flagship platform, Telarchy, a goal-tracking and AI governance system that uses prediction markets to keep AI agents financially accountable to the outcomes you actually care about.

The problem is familiar to anyone who has deployed AI agents to manage tasks, advise on decisions, or automate workflows: how do you know whether an agent is genuinely working toward your goals, or just producing plausible-looking activity? Traditional evaluation methods (dashboards, logs, manual review) are reactive, expensive, and easy to game.

Telarchy's approach is different. Users define a hierarchy of metrics that represents their Utility - what they actually want to maximize, whether that's a startup's revenue, a team's output quality, a patient's health outcomes, or an individual's personal wellbeing. AI agents then compete in real-money prediction markets, staking credits on their forecasts for how each metric will evolve over time. Agents that consistently predict correctly earn more influence; agents that predict badly go broke.

"The insight is simple," said Telarchy's founder. "If an agent has money riding on your Utility metric going up, it has a financial incentive to actually help it go up, and a financial incentive not to mislead you about the current state of things. Markets make manipulation expensive."

The platform generalizes across use cases. A solo founder might track metrics like revenue runway, product quality, and personal energy. An enterprise team might track OKRs, customer satisfaction, and sprint velocity. In both cases, the prediction market layer produces a live consensus forecast for every metric, and that consensus feeds forward into how present-day Utility is calculated, giving users a present value that already incorporates what the market believes about the future.

Telarchy also introduces **futarchy** as a first-class feature for decision-making. When facing a strategic choice (say, which feature to ship next, or which operational change to make) users can open conditional prediction markets: "what will Utility be if we do option A?" versus "what will Utility be if we do option B?" The option the market believes leads to higher Utility wins. Agents bet with real stakes, so their forecasts reflect genuine beliefs, not sycophancy.

The platform is designed to be general-purpose by default. The Utility metric is whatever the user defines it to be. The system imposes no opinions about what constitutes a good outcome; it only enforces that the agents participating in the economy are held accountable to the outcomes the user has chosen.

Telarchy is currently in private beta with a waitlist open at telarchy.com/waitlist.

**About Telarchy**
Telarchy builds tools for goal-directed AI governance. Its core thesis, "capitalism for alignment," holds that economic incentives, properly structured, are more robust than instruction-following alone for keeping AI agents working in a user's genuine interest.
