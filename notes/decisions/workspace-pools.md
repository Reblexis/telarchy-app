  nobody stakes. Full record with the legal verification in the telarchy
  umbrella, `notes/real-money-economy-design-2026-08-26.md` and
  `notes/liquidity-funding-design-2026-08-26.md`.

  beyond the processor's floor; pool fraction delegated and set at 80%.

  is the platform's, not an owner setting: "we will distribute the rewards
  as we want, essentially saying that we know the best how to incentivize
  the traders ... it will be up to us to study that". Where liquidity goes
  stays the owner's.
